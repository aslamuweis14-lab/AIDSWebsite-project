SYMPTOMS OF AIDS DISEASE

Persistent Fever: People living with AIDS may experience recurring or long-lasting fever because the weakened immune system cannot effectively fight infections resultimg to many infections entering the body and pathogens entering the body to affect much due to that white blood cells in the body immune have been destroyed much with the hiv virus.

Extreme Weight Loss: Rapid or unexplained weight loss is common in advanced HIV infection due to poor nutrient absorption and repeated illnesses in the body due to the body immune failing to resist the virus that results to the exessive loss in weight of the body rapidly.

Chronic Diarrhea: Persistent diarrhea lasting for weeks may occur as a result of infections affecting the digestive system ,the diarrhea may results to a very large loss of water in the body mainly reffered to as dehydration which require the patient to drink alot of water daily so as to compesate the loss of water in the body and to remain safe as the body requires water fo good survival and good well being .

Night Sweats: Many patients experience excessive sweating during sleep, especially when the body is fighting infections which usually cause the body to use much energy ,the HIV and Aids patient are required to have proper diet and eat very well to ensure that their bodies have enough energy for fighting the virus.

Frequent Infections: A weakened immune system allows diseases such as tuberculosis, pneumonia, and fungal infections to occur more often these are reffered often as opurtunistic diseases which takes place easily as the immune in the body becomes weakened by the virus and good proper treatment of these diseases frequently may help the patient to retain the good normal condition.

> Skin Problems: Rashes, sores, and unusual skin conditions may develop because the immune system can no longer properly defend the body
