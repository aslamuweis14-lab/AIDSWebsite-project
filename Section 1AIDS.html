AIDS OVERVIEW & CAUSES

Acquired Immune Deficiency Syndrome (AIDS) is a serious health condition caused by the Human Immunodeficiency Virus (HIV). The disease weakens the immune system, making the body unable to effectively fight infections and certain diseases. HIV attacks white blood cells called CD4 cells, which are important in protecting the body from germs. Without proper treatment, HIV gradually progresses into AIDS, the advanced stage of infection. Understanding the causes, transmission methods, symptoms, body effects, and preventive measures is very important in reducing the spread of the disease and protecting public health.

Causes of AIDS
