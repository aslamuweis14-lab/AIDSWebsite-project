PREVENTIVE MEASURES AGAINST AIDS

Avoiding Sharing Sharp Objects: People should avoid sharing needles, razors, and syringes because contaminated equipments with blood may carry the virus and get to transmit if the sharp tool is shared among various peolpe leading in getting affected with AIDS unkowingly ,In which people are advised to have personal tools like ;Razor blades and others to avoid risk of infections due to sharing of theses items which may be harmful in day to day life .

HIV Testing and Counseling: Regular HIV testing helps people know their status early and seek treatment or counseling when necessary and help to take early treatments if AIDS get to be found which can even prevent the disease in occuring if met in very early stages and handled wellas needed,due to regular checkup not only one may also get safe from AIDS infection but also being safe from various infections that were to occur in the body .

Safe Blood Transfusion: Hospitals and blood banks should carefully screen donated blood to ensure it is free from HIV infection and other various infections that may get to transfer to the one who will being receiving the donated blood this maintains the overall safety and ensure no effects of transmitting infections occurs during blood transmission.

Health Education and Awareness: Public education programs help communities understand HIV transmission and encourage safer behaviors ,health Education becomes very important as it gives Awareness to the people and makes them understands Aids in a good way which this reduces possibility of getting AIDS as the society gets familiar with the disease also Education is helpful as it gives directions to the society in proper living with the infected people well without discriminating and making them feeling bad and lonely

Avoid touching someones blood with bare hands: Touching someones blood with bare hands can transfer AIDS if the blood got to have the infections of Hiv ,it is encouraged to have gloves even when helpling someone with injuries or wouunds so as to avoid unnesesary infections that can be gotten and for the overall safety
