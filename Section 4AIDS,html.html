EFFECTS OF AIDS IN THE BODY

The AIDS disease may have various effcts in the body due to that the HIV virus causes much effects in the body

Weakening of the Immune System: AIDS destroys CD4 cells, making the immune system weak and reducing the bodys ability to resist infections which this condition puts the body in a very risk condition because the bodies shield has got affected resulting to easy entrance of many various infections as they meet the body protection to have been weakened, causing the person to experience many infections at once affecting someone health condition to be very weak.

Increased Opportunistic Infections: People with AIDS are vulnerable to infections that rarely affect healthy individuals, such as tuberculosis and certain fungal diseases this occurs due to the result that the body immune system responsible for protecting the body from infections got weakened with HIV virus .

> Damage to Body Organs: The disease may affect major organs including the lungs, brain, liver, and digestive system due to repeated infections that lead to serious effects when the infections get repeating frequently after the body protection has got worsening day by day leading the patient to get large costs due frequently continous treatments due to these damages that take place in the body .

Reduced Energy Levels: Patients often feel tired and weak because the body is constantly fighting illnesses and losing nutrients where the nutrients that are responsible for giving the body energy for survival and proper working they all concetrated and directed in fighting infections that are affecting the body as result it leads patients feeling tired and loosing energy even for normal activites and working.

Mental and Emotional Stress: Living with AIDS may lead to depression, anxiety, fear, and emotional distress due to social stigma and health challenges that some one may be experiencing ,and many people when they get AIDS get the fear that thay can loose thier lives any time which is usually not good as it inluences stess and unrestness that causes the body to be in a condition that is not good in proper wellbeing .

Reduced Life Expectancy: Without proper treatment, AIDS can significantly shorten a persons lifespan because of severe infections and complications that continously affect the body sytem in various ares that are important in survival ,due to many infections and diseases attacking much the body which when there is no proper treatment may largely affect the body and make the persons life to be in risk and someone him/herself to loose hope in living long life due to having critical conditions that body may be experiencing
